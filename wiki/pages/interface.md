## Control panel

- **Gold:** how much gold you have and how fast you gain it.
- **Troops:** your troops and your troop ceiling.
- **Attack ratio:** the share of your troops an attack sends. Sending more attacking troops than
  there are defenders means you lose fewer troops; sending fewer means you lose more. The effect
  stops growing at 2:1.

## Event panel

The latest events, requests and quick chat messages:

- **Attacks.** Incoming attacks and your own outgoing ones. Click a message to center the view on
  the attack, warhead or fleet. Click the red X to retreat, which costs a quarter of the attacking
  troops. A recalled fleet returns to its starting point and attacks there if the nebula has been
  captured since. Warheads can't be recalled once launched; Stargate shots can. A strike can be called off, but
  its troops are lost.
- **Alliance requests** to accept or reject. **Focus** moves the view to the player who sent it.
- **Quick chat** messages you sent and received.
- **Army reports:** how many troops your Armies' shots killed, or how many you lost to someone
  else's.

## Leaderboard

The top players of the round with their share of the nebulae, gold and troops. **Show All** lists
everyone; **Hide** hides it.

## Player info overlay

Hover over a territory to see who holds it: the player type (Human, Lord or Baron), a Lord's
attitude toward you (Hostile to Friendly), defending troops, gold, and how many Destroyers and
buildings they have.

## Radial menu

Right click (or touch on mobile) opens the radial menu; right click outside it to close it. From
the menu you can:

- open the **build menu**;
- open the **info menu** for the player under the cursor;
- send a **fleet** to the spot (only if your territory touches deep space);
- fire a **Stargate** shot or launch a warhead from the gate slot;
- open the **handshake** (the ally slot): **donate troops** or **gold** to anyone your
  Stargates reach, and **propose an alliance** (see [[alliances-and-diplomacy#donations-the-gate-network]]).
  On the Hegemon, donating troops reinforces the throne (see
  [[overthrowing-the-hegemon#reinforcing-the-throne]]);
- **attack** with the centre button: it always sends a ground attack.

## Info menu

The selected player's name, gold and troops, whether they have stopped trading with you, the
warheads they have sent you, and whether they are a traitor. From here you can:

- send an **alliance request**, or **break** an alliance;
- **stop** or **start trading** with them;
- place a **target mark** your allies can see;
- send a **quick chat** message or an **emoji**;
- **donate** troops or gold, when your Stargates reach them.

See [[alliances-and-diplomacy]].

## Build menu

Everything you can build, with its price and how many you already have. See [[buildings]],
[[armies-and-destroyers]] and [[stargates-and-warheads]].

## Player icons

| Icon             | Meaning                                      |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Crown            | The top player on the leaderboard            |
| Handshake        | Your ally                                    |
| Envelope         | This player has sent you an alliance request |
| Broken shield    | A traitor: this player attacked an ally      |
| Dollar stop sign | This player has stopped trading with you     |

## Options

- **Timer:** the round's clock.
- **Pause and speed:** solo games only.
- **Settings:** toggle the alternate view, emojis, dark mode, hidden names, the action on left
  click and more.
- **Exit:** leave the game.
