Challengers must cooperate enough to bring down the throne while racing each other to be the one
who takes it. Diplomacy is how you manage both.

## Alliances

- Send an **alliance request** from a player's info menu (or press <kbd>K</kbd> with the cursor
  over them). Requests appear in the event panel to be accepted or rejected.
- Allies can share gold and troops but can't attack each other.
- Trade with an ally pays the most: an ally's Population Centers and Spaceports pay 35,000 a
  supply delivery to each of you (see [[trade]]).
- **The Hegemon cannot ally with anyone.**

## Betrayal

Breaking an alliance (<kbd>L</kbd>) ends it, halts trade and weakens your defence:

- Trade between you is paused for 5 minutes, or until you become allies again, and others may stop
  trading with you too.
- Unless the other player was a traitor themselves, you are marked a **traitor** for 30 seconds:
  an icon appears above your name and you suffer a 50% defence penalty.
- Barons are less likely to ally with a traitor, and players will think twice.

## Donations: the gate network

Troops and gold travel through your [[stargates-and-warheads|Stargates]]. You may send either to
**any player who owns a tile within range of one of your finished Stargates**: no alliance is
needed, and the range is the only test. Upgrading a gate widens your circle of clients as well as
your reach. A transfer spends no gate charge.

- **In flight:** a transfer leaves you at once and flies, in your colour, from your gate nearest
  the recipient to their nearest tile (the Hegemon's from the Dark Star). Troops and gold for the
  Hegemon always land on the Dark Star itself, never on ground it holds beyond the moat. It arrives only when it
  lands. If that tile changes hands while it is in the air, even if the recipient takes it back,
  the troops or gold come home to you when it lands. Troops beyond the recipient's room come home
  too. A transfer in flight cannot be recalled.

- **Where:** right-click the player and open the **handshake** (the ally slot): **Donate troops**
  (your attack ratio's share), **Donate gold** (opens the gold dialog) and **Propose alliance**.
  A greyed item tells you why: out of reach, on cooldown, or switched off in this game.
- **Cooldown:** after a donation you must wait a few seconds before sending the same player
  more.
- **Lobby settings:** a game may switch off gold or troop donations between challengers.
- **To the throne:** you may send the Hegemon troops or gold only if one of your gates reaches
  the Dark Star. Troops go into its pool while it has room; while someone is invading, they join
  the counter-attack as they land. See [[overthrowing-the-hegemon#reinforcing-the-throne]].
- **To the invader:** the player holding the Dark Star can be supplied like anyone in reach.
- **From the throne:** the Hegemon reaches everyone, with troops and gold, whatever the lobby
  settings say.
- **Alliances** are not limited by range: you may propose, extend or break one from anywhere.

## Embargoes

**Stop trading** with a player and you will no longer send them gold, or receive theirs, by
supply delivery or freighter. Trading resumes only when you both click **Start trading**. Trade
also stops automatically between players at war, for 5 minutes.

## Signals

- **Target mark:** place a target on a player, visible to all your allies, to coordinate an attack.
- **Quick chat:** pick a category and a phrase (and a player, if the phrase names one) and send it.
- **Emojis:** send a quick emoji to any player.
