**Antimatter Warhead.** The large warhead: {{hCost}} gold, a 100-tile blast. See
[[stargates-and-warheads]].

**Army.** A defensive land unit that patrols your nebula and kills a share of attacking troops.
See [[armies-and-destroyers]].

**Attack ratio.** The share of your troops an attack, fleet or Stargate shot sends.

**Baron.** A simple AI player. See [[lords-and-barons]].

**Beaching.** A pirate landing their corsair on a coast to claim a foothold and return as a
challenger. See [[piracy]].

**Bubble.** The nebula a Hegemon's strike holds while it lasts. Nobody can take it back; when
the strike ends it pops and the nebula becomes neutral. See [[combat#strikes]].

**Challenger.** Every player except the Hegemon.

**Corsair.** A pirate's one ship. See [[piracy]].

**Dark Star.** The supermassive black hole at the centre of every map: a disc of nebula, the
Hegemon's seat and the only nebula it can own. See [[overthrowing-the-hegemon]].

**Deep space.** The space between the nebulae, crossed by fleets, Destroyers and freighters. See
[[galaxy]].

**Defense Post.** A building that makes nearby nebula expensive to take. See [[buildings]].

**Destroyer.** A deep-space warship launched from a Spaceport's harbour. See
[[armies-and-destroyers]].

**Embargo.** Stopping trade with a player. See [[alliances-and-diplomacy#embargoes]].

**Fleet.** Troops crossing deep space to land on a coast.

**Freighter.** A trade ship sailing between two players' Spaceports and paying both. See
[[trade]].

**Gift.** A building the Hegemon places or upgrades on a challenger's nebula at its own cost. The
host owns it and cannot refuse it.

**Harbour.** The deep space nearest a Spaceport, where its freighters put to space and its
Destroyers launch and repair.

**Hegemon.** The ruler of the Dark Star: immensely strong, unable to expand. One per round. See
[[hegemon]].

**Hold.** The troops a corsair carries; lost if it sinks, sent ashore when it beaches.

**Industrial Zone.** A building that builds a trade network and sends supply deliveries. Each
level supports one Army. See [[buildings]].

**Invader.** The one challenger currently allowed to hold Dark Star tiles.

**Killing blow.** The conquest that takes the Hegemon's last tile. Its owner wins the round.

**Lord.** A smart AI player placed by the map. See [[lords-and-barons]].

**Moat.** The ring of deep space around the Dark Star.

**Nebula** (plural **nebulae**). The ground of the galaxy: every tile of territory is nebula. See
[[galaxy]].

**Nuclear Warhead.** The small warhead: {{nukeCost}} gold, a 30-tile blast.

**Overthrow.** Eliminating the Hegemon.

**Pirate.** An eliminated challenger sailing on as a corsair. See [[piracy]].

**Population Center.** A building that raises your troop ceiling. See [[buildings]].

**Punitive strike.** The Hegemon's answer to a warhead launch: a levy on every challenger that
lands on the launching gate. See [[hegemon#punitive-strikes]].

**Reign.** The consecutive rounds a player holds the same throne. See [[thrones]].

**Shadow tax.** The Hegemon's income: {{tax}}% of everything the challengers earn, taken from
nobody.

**Shield Array.** A building that keeps other players' warheads off everything within
{{shieldR}} tiles. See [[stargates-and-warheads#shield-array]].

**Spaceport.** A building that sends freighters and launches Destroyers. See [[buildings]].

**Stargate.** A building that fires troops at any tile in range and launches warheads. See
[[stargates-and-warheads]].

**Strike.** The Hegemon's attack on challenger territory. See [[hegemon#strikes]].

**Supply delivery.** A shipment riding an Industrial Zone's trade network, paying gold at every
Population Center and Spaceport it passes. See [[trade]].

**Throne.** A public lobby that runs rounds back to back, and the seat of the Hegemon. See
[[thrones]].

**Trade lane.** A straight link between two stations on a trade network.

**Tyranny.** The rising cost of striking a small player; at the floor they cannot be struck. See
[[hegemon#tyranny]].

**Void.** Impassable space. Nothing crosses it.
