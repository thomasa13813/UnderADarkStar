Every challenger wins the same way: by taking the Dark Star's last tile. This page covers what
that takes.

## The Dark Star

- The Dark Star is a solid disc at the centre of every map, separated from the rest by a ring of
  deep space: **the moat**. It is the only nebula the Hegemon can ever own.
- Nothing can be built on Dark Star tiles, by anyone, ever.
- Warheads do nothing to Dark Star tiles. The only way to take them is to land troops: by
  **fleet** across the moat, or by **Stargate shot**.
- Taking a tile from the Hegemon costs more troops and more time than an ordinary conquest.
- Holding Dark Star tiles gives a challenger nothing: no troop capacity, no income. Losing them
  costs the Hegemon a fixed share of its troops per tile; otherwise its strength does not depend
  on how much of the disc it holds.

## One invader at a time

Only one challenger at a time may hold Dark Star tiles: **the invader**.

- The slot is claimed when a fleet or Stargate shot lands on the disc, and held for as long as the
  invader holds any tile there.
- Anyone else's fleet or shot bound for the disc turns back the moment the slot is taken, troops
  home. A Stargate shot at the disc is refused at launch while someone else holds the slot.
- There is no cooldown: an evicted challenger may land again as soon as the slot is free.

Because only one challenger can be on the disc, the killing blow is never ambiguous.

## A stalled invasion fights back

If the invader has gained no Dark Star tile for {{stall}} seconds, all of the Hegemon's troop
income is sent against them automatically until they are evicted. This holds for a human or an AI
Hegemon alike. A sliver of the disc cannot be sat on: an invasion has to keep moving.

## Reinforcing the throne

Anyone whose [[stargates-and-warheads|Stargate]] reaches the Dark Star may donate troops (or gold)
to the Hegemon while its pool has room, with no alliance needed and the lobby's donation setting
ignored. While someone is invading, every troop donated joins the counter-attack against them at
once.

The invader can be supplied too: anyone whose gate reaches the invader's nebula may send them
troops and gold, so a coalition can feed an invasion through its gates.

Expect rivals to do exactly this when you are close to winning. The challenger who is not
invading has every reason to stop the one who is.

## Keep a homeland

An invader must keep territory outside the Dark Star. The moment it holds no nebula beyond the
moat it is eliminated, and its Dark Star tiles return to the Hegemon at once. Whoever took its
last nebula has the kill.

## Ways in

- **Fleet.** Send a fleet across the moat to the rim of the disc. You need a coast on the moat's
  body of deep space.
- **Stargate.** A gate whose range reaches the disc can fire your troops straight onto it,
  skipping the moat. Range grows by {{gatePer}} tiles with every level, so a gate far from the
  centre can be upgraded until it reaches. Nothing intercepts a gate shot. See
  [[stargates-and-warheads]].

## Tips for an invasion

- Time it. The Hegemon's pool refills at a flat rate. Right after it has spent troops on a strike,
  or on another invader, it is weaker.
- Bring enough to keep gaining tiles. The stall rule punishes an invasion that stops.
- Watch your home front. While you invade, your neighbours may go for your homeland, and losing
  the last of it ends your invasion and your round.
- [[armies-and-destroyers|Armies]] and Defense Posts at home make it costly for rivals to take
  advantage of you.
