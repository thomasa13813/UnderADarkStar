Gold pays for every building, unit and warhead. A challenger has three sources of it: base
income, supply deliveries and freighters. The exact figures are in the
[[buildings#4-income-compared|buildings reference]].

## Base income

Every challenger earns a steady **6,000 gold a second**. Barons earn half that. The Hegemon has no
base income: it takes a {{tax}}% **shadow tax** on everything the challengers earn, without
deducting anything from them (see [[hegemon]]).

**Gold is for spending.** The Hegemon's **tithe** takes everything above {{titheReserve}} from
everyone under its ring when it lands (see [[hegemon#tithes]]). When a ring falls on your
nebula, spend, or give your gold away to anyone your Stargates reach, before it comes down.

## Trade lanes and supply deliveries

An **Industrial Zone** builds a trade network: straight **trade lanes** to Population Centers,
Spaceports and other Industrial Zones within reach, **anyone's**, not just your own.

- **Lanes are straight lines**, in the simulation as well as on screen. A lane fails if it crosses
  void, the Dark Star's disc, or open deep space. Only a narrow strait of shoreline space can be
  crossed.
- **Supply deliveries** set out from Industrial Zones and ride the network to a random Population
  Center or Spaceport. Each level of a zone is one more chance a second to send one.
- **Gold is paid at every Population Center and Spaceport the delivery passes**, and stops that
  belong to other players pay **more** than your own, to both of you:

| Stop belongs to                | The delivery's owner gets | The stop's owner gets |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------- | --------------------- |
| You                            | 10,000                    | -                     |
| A teammate or a neutral player | 25,000                    | 25,000                |
| An ally                        | 35,000                    | 35,000                |

Trading with your neighbours pays. That is a reason to keep the peace with them, and to ally.

## Spaceports, harbours and freighters

A **Spaceport** can be placed anywhere in your territory. Its **harbour** is the nearest deep
space reachable over nebula: that is where its freighters put to space and where Destroyers are
launched and repaired.

- **Freighters** sail from your spaceports to **other players'** spaceports that share a body of
  space. You never trade with yourself.
- A freighter crawls overland at half speed to its harbour, crosses space at full speed, and
  crawls in at the far end.
- On arrival **both** spaceports' owners receive the full payment, which rises steeply with the
  distance travelled: short hauls pay very little, routes of 300 tiles and more pay well.
- Each level of a spaceport is one more chance a second to send a freighter.

## Stopping trade

- Trade with a player stops automatically when you attack them or are attacked by them. It
  resumes after 5 minutes, or if you become allies.
- You can stop trading with a player yourself with **Stop trading**. It starts again only when
  you both click **Start trading**.
- A delivery never pays at, or travels to, a player either side has stopped trading with, and no
  freighter sails between you.
- A player who has stopped trading with you shows a dollar stop sign.

## Raiders

- A **Destroyer** can capture another player's freighter in open deep space (never on nebula or
  just off the coast). The captured freighter pays only its captor.
- A freighter caught by the Hegemon's Destroyers is simply lost.
- A **pirate corsair** scuttles freighters and takes gold and troops for itself (see [[piracy]]).

Long routes across open space pay the most and are the most exposed. Destroyers of your own guard
them (see [[armies-and-destroyers]]).
