## The idea in one paragraph

Every round has one **Hegemon**, who holds the Dark Star at the centre of the map, and many
**challengers**. As a challenger you grow from a small foothold into an empire, then try to be the
one who eliminates the Hegemon. You are racing the clock as well as each other: if the Hegemon
survives {{minutes}} minutes, it wins. See [[the-round]] for the exact rules.

## Play the tutorial first

The quickest way to learn is the in-game tutorial: on the home page, choose **Tutorial**. It
starts a solo game with a step-by-step guide through spawning, attacking, building and
warheads. Solo games are played against AI players: [[lords-and-barons|Lords and Barons]], and
an AI Hegemon unless you choose to play the Hegemon yourself.

## Your first minute

1. **Pick a spawn.** At the start of the round, click a spot of nebula to choose where you begin.
   Somewhere with room to grow, away from strong neighbours, is a good start.
2. **The opening.** When the spawn phase ends, every challenger is launched from the Dark Star to
   their spawn. Nothing moves and the clock holds until the last one has landed. You are protected
   for a few seconds after landing.
3. **Expand.** Click unclaimed nebula next to your territory to send troops into it. Neutral
   nebula is cheap to take; other players' nebula is defended by their troops.
4. **Build.** Open the build menu and raise your first buildings: a
   [[buildings#3-1-population-center|Population Center]] for more troops, an
   [[trade|Industrial Zone]] for gold.

## Troops and gold

- **Troops** are your army and your population at once. They regrow on their own, fastest when
  you are a little under half your **troop ceiling** and not at all when you are at it. Your
  ceiling grows with your territory and with every level of Population Center.
- **Gold** pays for buildings, units and warheads. Every challenger earns a steady base income,
  and trade earns much more: see [[trade]].
- The **attack ratio** slider sets what share of your troops an attack sends. Sending more troops
  than the defender has makes the attack cheaper; the advantage stops growing at about 5:3. See
  [[combat]] for everything else that sets the price of a tile.

## Attacking

- **Over nebula:** click an enemy's territory next to yours.
- **Across deep space:** send a **fleet** to the coast you want. Your territory must touch deep
  space.
- **Past a front, or onto the Dark Star:** a [[stargates-and-warheads|Stargate]] fires your troops
  at any tile in range.
- You can call an attack back from the event panel, at the cost of a quarter of its troops.
  Fleets and Stargate shots can be recalled; warheads cannot. The Hegemon can call off a
  strike, but its troops are lost.

## Winning

Only one thing wins a round for a challenger: the **killing blow** on the Hegemon. That means
invading the Dark Star, which only one challenger may do at a time. Read
[[overthrowing-the-hegemon]] before you try. Win, and you are the Hegemon of the next round.
