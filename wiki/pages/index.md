**Under a Dark Star** is a free, real-time territorial strategy game played in the browser. One
player, the **Hegemon**, rules from the Dark Star at the centre of the galaxy: enormously strong,
but unable to expand. Everyone else is a **challenger** who starts small, spreads across the
nebulae, builds an economy and an arsenal, and races the others to land the blow that topples
the throne. Whoever takes the Dark Star's last tile wins the round and becomes the next Hegemon.
If the {{minutes}}-minute timer runs out first, the Hegemon wins and keeps the throne.

New to the galaxy? Start with [[getting-started]], then read [[the-round]]. If you have just
taken the throne, read [[hegemon]].
