## Nebulae

**Nebulae** (singular **nebula**) are regions of space dense enough to form planets: the ground of
the game. Every tile of territory is nebula. You spread over nebula by attacking next to your
border, and the more of it you hold, the higher your troop ceiling and the more room you have to
build.

Unclaimed nebula is neutral and cheap to take. A tile that becomes nobody's, for instance when a
Hegemon's strike pops, is neutral again.

## Deep space

**Deep space** separates the nebulae. Nobody owns it.

- **Fleets** carry troops across deep space to land on another coast. Your territory must touch
  deep space to send one.
- **Destroyers** patrol it, and **freighters** cross it between spaceports (see [[trade]]).
- **Straits:** a narrow strait of shoreline space can be crossed by a trade lane. Open deep space
  cannot.

## Void

True **void** is impassable. Nothing crosses it: not fleets, not trade lanes.

## The Dark Star and the moat

At the centre of every map is the **Dark Star**, a supermassive black hole and the seat of the
Hegemon: a solid disc of nebula surrounded by a ring of deep space called **the moat**.

- The Dark Star is the only nebula the Hegemon can own, and nothing can be built on it.
- Warheads do nothing to its tiles.
- Freighters and trade lanes never cross the disc.

See [[overthrowing-the-hegemon]] for how challengers take it.

## Getting past the terrain

A [[stargates-and-warheads|Stargate]] shot flies in an arc and lands on any tile in range that is
not yours: a beachhead behind any coastline or front line, with no fleet needed.
