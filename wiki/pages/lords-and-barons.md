The galaxy is feudal. Above everyone sits the **Hegemon**. Below it stand the **Lords**, and
beneath them the **Barons**. Hovering over a territory shows which kind of player holds it: Human,
Lord or Baron.

## Lords

Lords are the smarter AI players. Each map places its own set of Lords (see [[maps]]).

- **They play by your rules.** Lords build with the same rules and prices as you, trade, launch
  Destroyers, deploy Armies and fire Stargates and warheads.
- **They go for the throne.** A Lord invades the Dark Star when the slot is free and its troops
  measure up against the Hegemon's current strength, and it grows bolder as the timer runs down.
  An AI-only round can end in an overthrow.
- **They defend.** Under a land attack, Lords (except on Easy) send their Armies to the front and
  build Defense Posts when the attack is heavy.
- **Attitude.** A Lord has an attitude toward you, from Hostile to Friendly, shown when you hover
  over its territory.
- **One Shield Array.** Once a Lord owns at least one of every other building, it builds one
  Shield Array where it covers the most of its buildings.
- **Warheads.** A Lord saves towards one warhead. It fires a Gamma Ray Burst at its target
  wherever the blast spares its own and its friends' ground, whoever else it hits, and otherwise
  an Antimatter Reaction if that fits on its target's ground.

### Difficulty

The lobby's **Lord difficulty** sets how smart Lords are and how many troops they can field
compared with a human:

| Difficulty     | Smarts     | Troop ceiling       |
| -------------- | ---------- | ------------------- |
| **Easy**       | Dumb       | 50% of a human's    |
| **Medium**     | Average    | 75% of a human's    |
| **Hard**       | Smart      | The same as a human |
| **Impossible** | Very smart | 125% of a human's   |

The [[buildings#6-how-lords-build|buildings reference]] details how Lords choose what to build.

## Barons

Barons are the simple bots that fill out the map.

- They are on the map from the start of the round; they do not fly in from the Dark Star.
- They earn half a challenger's base income, and their troop ceiling is a third of a human's.
- They do not build, and they never invade the Dark Star or turn pirate.
- They are less likely to ally with a traitor.

Barons are the easy meat of the early game: taking their nebula is a fast way to grow.
