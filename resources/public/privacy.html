Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

Under a Dark Star (the "game") is run by January Games, LLC, a Minnesota limited liability company ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect when you visit darkstar.january-games.com or play the game, why, who else handles it, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • You can play as a guest without giving us anything about yourself.
  • If you sign in with Discord or Google, we keep that service's ID for you and a display name. We do not receive your email address or password.
  • Your account name and leaderboard results are public. Game records are kept for 30 days.
  • We do not sell your information, show ads, or use tracking cookies. We count visits with Cloudflare's Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and shows us only totals.
  • You can delete your account yourself at any time, from Settings → Account. Email hello@january-games.com to see or correct your data.

1. What we collect

When you play as a guest

  • A random browser ID, created and stored in your browser. It gives you the same guest name between games (for example Anon48213) and lets the server recognise your connection during a game. It is not linked to your name or any other account.
  • Your settings (language, sound, display options, the name you last typed, favourite maps and similar). These stay in your browser and are not sent to us except as part of joining a game.

When you sign in with Discord or Google

Signing in is optional. When you choose it, the provider tells us your account's unique ID and your display name. We ask for nothing more: Discord's "identify" permission, and Google's basic profile. We store:

  • the provider's ID for your account, and which provider it is;
  • your account name and its four-digit tag (taken from the provider at first sign-in), and when it was last changed;
  • when the account was created;
  • sign-in sessions: a scrambled (hashed) copy of your sign-in cookie, with when it was created and when it expires;
  • moderation details, if any (for example a ban and its reason).

We do not receive or store your email address, password, profile picture or contacts.

When you play

  • Game records. For each multiplayer game, the server keeps a record of the players' in-game names, their moves and the result, for 30 days. We use it to investigate bugs, cheating and disputed results.
  • Leaderboard results. For signed-in players in public throne games, we record which rounds you played, whether you were the Hegemon and whether you won. From these we work out your victories, reigns, longest reign and games played.

Technical information

  • Your IP address. The server uses it while you are connected to limit abuse (rate limits, duplicate connections, vote checks). It is sent to Cloudflare's Turnstile bot check when you join a game. Our server logs record only a shortened, anonymised form of it. We do not store IP addresses in our account database.
  • Server logs of requests and errors. They are overwritten automatically as they fill and are cleared whenever the server is updated.
  • Network data. All traffic passes through Cloudflare, which sees your IP address and browser details in order to deliver and protect the site.
  • Visit statistics. Our pages load Cloudflare's Web Analytics script, which reports each page view: the page's address, the site that sent you there, your browser and device type, your country (worked out from your IP address) and how quickly the page loaded. It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. We see only totals, such as visits per day, popular pages, referring sites and countries; we cannot pick out an individual visitor.

When you contact us

If you email us, we keep your message and email address for as long as we need them to deal with it.

2. How we use it

  • to run the game and keep you signed in;
  • to show the leaderboard and the names of players in a game;
  • to stop cheating, bots, spam and abuse, and to enforce our rules;
  • to find and fix bugs;
  • to count visits and see which pages people use, so we can improve the site;
  • to answer you when you write to us;
  • to meet legal obligations.

We do not sell or rent personal information. We do not use it for advertising, build profiles of you, or use it to train AI models.

3. What is public

  • Your account name and tag, and your leaderboard results, are visible to anyone.
  • The names you play under are visible to the other players in that game.
  • A game's record can be opened by anyone with its link for 30 days. It shows in-game names and moves, never browser IDs.

4. Cookies and browser storage

We use only what the game needs:

We do not use advertising, analytics or social-media tracking cookies. Our visit statistics (section 1) use no cookies or browser storage. Because everything above is needed for the site to work or remembers your own choices, we do not show a cookie banner.

5. Who else handles your information

We use a small number of service providers, each for one job:

  • Cloudflare carries all traffic to the site, protects it from attacks, runs the Turnstile bot check and counts visits for us (Web Analytics).
  • Our hosting provider rents us the server that runs the game and stores the account database.
  • Discord and Google, only if you choose to sign in with them. Their own privacy policies cover what they do.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect people's safety or our rights, or to a successor if the game or January Games, LLC is sold or reorganised (this policy would continue to apply to your information).

6. Sign in with Google

If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account ID and name. We use them only to create and recognise your game account and to suggest its first name. We store the ID and the account name described above, and nothing else from Google. We do not share Google user data with anyone, use it for advertising, sell it, or use it to train AI models. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

You can remove the game's access at any time from your Google account's third-party connections (for Discord: User Settings → Authorized Apps). Removing access does not delete your game account; you can do that yourself (section 8).

8. Your choices and rights

  • You can always play as a guest, without an account.
  • You can clear the guest ID and settings by clearing your browser data.
  • You can delete your account yourself at any time: sign in, open Settings → Account and choose Delete account. It happens at once. Your sign-ins, sessions, account name and leaderboard numbers are erased, and you leave the leaderboard. Rounds you played stay only as anonymous history, not linked to you. Game records you appear in are deleted on their normal 30-day schedule. If you sign in again later, you start a new, empty account.
  • You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about your account, or to correct it. If you cannot sign in, or your account is banned, you can also ask us to delete it. Email hello@january-games.com with your account name and tag. We may ask for information to confirm that the account is yours.

We answer within 45 days. Depending on where you live, including Minnesota and other US states, the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, you may have further rights: to object to or restrict some uses, to receive your data in a portable form, to appeal our decision on a request, and to complain to a data protection authority. Minnesota residents may contact the Minnesota Attorney General if we deny an appeal. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

9. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and you must be at least 13 to create an account (or older, where local law requires a parent's consent below a higher age). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

10. Security

The site is served only over HTTPS. Sign-in cookies are stored only in hashed form, we keep as little as we can, and access to the server is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, but if a breach affects your information we will tell you as the law requires.

11. Where your information is processed

We are based in the United States. Your information is processed in the United States and in other countries where our service providers operate. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we rely on these legal bases: providing the service you asked for (your account, games and the leaderboard), our legitimate interests in keeping the game fair and secure and in understanding how the site is used (visit statistics), and meeting legal obligations.

12. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change, and change the date at the top. If a change is significant, for example if we start showing advertising, we will announce it on the site before it takes effect and ask for your consent wherever the law requires it.
