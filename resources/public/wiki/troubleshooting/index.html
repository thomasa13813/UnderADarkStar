Troubleshooting

Fixes for slow, choppy or broken Under a Dark Star games - browser, GPU and hardware acceleration checks, and where to report a bug.

On this page
  1. The diagnostics panel
  2. If the game is slow or choppy
  3. Still stuck?

The diagnostics panel

The game has a built-in diagnostics panel that shows your browser, operating system, GPU and how the game is being drawn, with a button to copy it all.

Open the diagnostics panel

If the game is slow or choppy

  • Use a Chromium-based browser (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera). Under a Dark Star runs best on them.
  • Turn on hardware acceleration in your browser's settings. Without it the game is drawn by the processor instead of the graphics card, which is far slower. If the diagnostics panel shows Software rendering, this is the problem.
  • Check the GPU. On a laptop with two graphics chips, make sure the panel shows the dedicated one, if you have one.
  • Turn off power saving in your browser and, on a laptop, plug in. Power saving mode slows the game down.
  • Press Alt + R in game to reset the graphics settings.

Still stuck?

Copy the diagnostics panel's information and tell us what happened:

  • on the Discord server, the place for bug reports;
  • or by email at hello@january-games.com.
