Under a Dark Star Wiki

Rules, strategy and reference for Under a Dark Star, the free browser strategy game where one player rules the galaxy and everyone else tries to overthrow them.

Under a Dark Star is a free, real-time territorial strategy game played in the browser. One player, the Hegemon, rules from the Dark Star at the centre of the galaxy: enormously strong, but unable to expand. Everyone else is a challenger who starts small, spreads across the nebulae, builds an economy and an arsenal, and races the others to land the blow that topples the throne. Whoever takes the Dark Star's last tile wins the round and becomes the next Hegemon. If the 20-minute timer runs out first, the Hegemon wins and keeps the throne.

New to the galaxy? Start with Getting started, then read The round. If you have just taken the throne, read The Hegemon.
