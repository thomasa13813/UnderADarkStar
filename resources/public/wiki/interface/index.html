The game screen

A guide to the Under a Dark Star interface - the control panel, event panel, leaderboard, radial menu, info menu, build menu and player icons.

Control panel

  • Gold: how much gold you have and how fast you gain it.
  • Troops: your troops and your troop ceiling.
  • Attack ratio: the share of your troops an attack sends. Sending more attacking troops than there are defenders means you lose fewer troops; sending fewer means you lose more. The effect stops growing at 2:1.

Event panel

The latest events, requests and quick chat messages:

  • Attacks. Incoming attacks and your own outgoing ones. Click a message to center the view on the attack, warhead or fleet. Click the red X to retreat, which costs a quarter of the attacking troops. A recalled fleet returns to its starting point and attacks there if the nebula has been captured since. Warheads can't be recalled once launched; Stargate shots can. A strike can be called off, but its troops are lost.
  • Alliance requests to accept or reject. Focus moves the view to the player who sent it.
  • Quick chat messages you sent and received.
  • Army reports: how many troops your Armies' shots killed, or how many you lost to someone else's.

Leaderboard

The top players of the round with their share of the nebulae, gold and troops. Show All lists everyone; Hide hides it.

Player info overlay

Hover over a territory to see who holds it: the player type (Human, Lord or Baron), a Lord's attitude toward you (Hostile to Friendly), defending troops, gold, and how many Destroyers and buildings they have.

Radial menu

Right click (or touch on mobile) opens the radial menu; right click outside it to close it. From the menu you can:

  • open the build menu;
  • open the info menu for the player under the cursor;
  • send a fleet to the spot (only if your territory touches deep space);
  • fire a Stargate shot or launch a warhead from the gate slot;
  • donate troops or gold to an ally;
  • reinforce the throne: send troops to the Hegemon (see Overthrowing the Hegemon).

Info menu

The selected player's name, gold and troops, whether they have stopped trading with you, the warheads they have sent you, and whether they are a traitor. From here you can:

  • send an alliance request, or break an alliance;
  • stop or start trading with them;
  • place a target mark your allies can see;
  • send a quick chat message or an emoji;
  • with an ally, donate troops or gold.

See Alliances and diplomacy.

Build menu

Everything you can build, with its price and how many you already have. See Buildings, Armies and Destroyers and Stargates, warheads and shields.

Options

  • Timer: the round's clock.
  • Pause and speed: solo games only.
  • Settings: toggle the alternate view, emojis, dark mode, hidden names, the action on left click and more.
  • Exit: leave the game.
