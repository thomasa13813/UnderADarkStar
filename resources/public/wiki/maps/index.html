Maps

The galaxy maps of Under a Dark Star, each built around the Dark Star at its centre, with their sizes and number of Lords.

Every map in Under a Dark Star is a galaxy built around the Dark Star at its centre, with the moat around it and nebulae, deep space and void beyond. There are 23 maps:

Size is the map's width and height in tiles. Lords is how many Lords the map places when Lords are enabled (see Lords and Barons).

After a public round, the winner picks the next map (see Thrones and lobbies). In a private lobby or a solo game you choose it yourself.
