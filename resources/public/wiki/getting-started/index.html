Getting started

Your first round of Under a Dark Star - spawning, expanding, troops and gold, attacking, building, and how to win.

On this page
  1. The idea in one paragraph
  2. Play the tutorial first
  3. Your first minute
  4. Troops and gold
  5. Attacking
  6. Winning

The idea in one paragraph

Every round has one Hegemon, who holds the Dark Star at the centre of the map, and many challengers. As a challenger you grow from a small foothold into an empire, then try to be the one who eliminates the Hegemon. You are racing the clock as well as each other: if the Hegemon survives 20 minutes, it wins. See The round for the exact rules.

Play the tutorial first

The quickest way to learn is the in-game tutorial: on the home page, choose Tutorial. It starts a solo game with a step-by-step guide through spawning, attacking, building and warheads. Solo games are played against AI players: Lords and Barons, and an AI Hegemon unless you choose to play the Hegemon yourself.

Your first minute

  1. Pick a spawn. At the start of the round, click a spot of nebula to choose where you begin. Somewhere with room to grow, away from strong neighbours, is a good start.
  2. The opening. When the spawn phase ends, every challenger is launched from the Dark Star to their spawn. Nothing moves and the clock holds until the last one has landed. You are protected for a few seconds after landing.
  3. Expand. Click unclaimed nebula next to your territory to send troops into it. Neutral nebula is cheap to take; other players' nebula is defended by their troops.
  4. Build. Open the build menu and raise your first buildings: a Population Center for more troops, an Industrial Zone for gold.

Troops and gold

  • Troops are your army and your population at once. They regrow on their own, faster the further you are below your troop ceiling. Your ceiling grows with your territory and with every level of Population Center.
  • Gold pays for buildings, units and warheads. Every challenger earns a steady base income, and trade earns much more: see Trade and the economy.
  • The attack ratio slider sets what share of your troops an attack sends. Sending more troops than the defender has makes the attack cheaper; the advantage stops growing at 2:1.

Attacking

  • Over nebula: click an enemy's territory next to yours.
  • Across deep space: send a fleet to the coast you want. Your territory must touch deep space.
  • Past a front, or onto the Dark Star: a Stargate fires your troops at any tile in range.
  • You can call an attack back from the event panel, at the cost of a quarter of its troops. Fleets, Stargate shots and the Hegemon's strikes can be recalled; warheads cannot.

Winning

Only one thing wins a round for a challenger: the killing blow on the Hegemon. That means invading the Dark Star, which only one challenger may do at a time. Read Overthrowing the Hegemon before you try. Win, and you are the Hegemon of the next round.
