Controls and hotkeys

Every mouse action and default keyboard shortcut in Under a Dark Star - attacking, building, fleets, Destroyers, Armies, camera and game speed.

On this page
  1. Mouse
  2. Keyboard
  3. Build keys

These are the default keys. You can rebind them in Settings.

Mouse

ActionWhat it does
Left clickAttack the tile, or open the menu, depending on your "action on left click" setting
Right click (touch on mobile)Open the radial menu (see The game screen)
Shift + left clickAttack, when left click is set to open the menu
Ctrl + left clickOpen the build menu (⌘ on a Mac)
Alt + left clickOpen the emote menu
Middle clickUpgrade the nearest building automatically
Shift + mouse wheelDecrease or increase the attack ratio
Shift + dragSelect several Destroyers and Armies
Mouse wheelZoom

Keyboard

KeyWhat it does
EscClose a menu, or cancel a build preview
EnterBuild the unit under the cursor
SpaceAlternate view (terrain / territories)
MToggle the coordinate grid
USwap a Nuclear or Antimatter Warhead's arc direction
CCenter the camera on your territory
W A S DMove the camera
Q / EZoom out / in
T / YDecrease / increase the attack ratio
BSend a fleet to the tile under the cursor
GGround attack on the tile under the cursor
Shift + RRetaliate against your most recent attacker (only while under attack)
KRequest an alliance with the player under the cursor
LBreak the alliance with the player under the cursor
FSelect all your Destroyers
PPause or resume (solo games)
, / .Game speed down / up (solo games)
Alt + RReset graphics

Build keys

Press a number to build the matching unit under the cursor. Each icon in the build bar shows its digit.

KeyBuilds
1Population Center
2Industrial Zone
3Spaceport
4Defense Post
5Shield Array
6Stargate
7Destroyer
8Nuclear Warhead
9Antimatter Warhead
0Army
