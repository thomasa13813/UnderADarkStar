Piracy

Eliminated in Under a Dark Star? Embrace piracy - sail a corsair, raid freighters for gold and troops, and beach on a coast to come back as a challenger.

On this page
  1. Embracing piracy
  2. Raiding
  3. Beaching: the way back
  4. Sinking
  5. Winning as a pirate

Elimination does not have to be the end of your round. Piracy is on in every public throne round; a private lobby or a solo game may switch it off.

Embracing piracy

  • A challenger who has been eliminated may embrace piracy from the death screen, as often as they fall.
  • The Hegemon, Lords and Barons never turn pirate.
  • A pirate commands one ship, the corsair, and nothing else: no territory, no building, no alliances.
  • Click deep space outside the Dark Star and its moat to launch the corsair, 15 seconds after your elimination.
  • At sea the corsair flies no name and no colours: everyone sees only a pirate ship.

Raiding

  • Click deep space to send the corsair there on patrol; click nebula to send it ashore.
  • On patrol it hunts freighters. Each one it catches is scuttled, the pirate pockets 10,000 gold, and 100 troops go into the hold, which carries at most 2,500.
  • It never fires on fleets but trades shots with Destroyers in range.
  • It heals slowly anywhere in deep space and never docks.

Beaching: the way back

Beaching the corsair claims 100 tiles around the landing for free (buildings excluded) and sends the hold ashore as an attack on the coast's owner. The pirate is a challenger again, in their own name and colours, with no spawn protection.

You cannot land on the Dark Star, on a player still under spawn protection, or on a teammate's coast, and landings stop in the round's last 60 seconds.

Sinking

  • A sunk corsair loses its whole hold; the raid gold stays with the player.
  • The next corsair launches after a wait that doubles with every sinking, from 15 seconds up to 120.

Winning as a pirate

A pirate at sea cannot win the round. A returned challenger can, like anyone. A pirate who leaves the game has the corsair scuttled, and a returned challenger who falls again may embrace piracy again.
