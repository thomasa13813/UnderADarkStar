Armies and Destroyers

Units in Under a Dark Star - Armies that defend your nebula against land attacks, Destroyers that patrol deep space, and how many of each you can field.

On this page
  1. Support: how many you may field
  2. Armies
  3. Destroyers

Challengers field two kinds of unit, each supported by a building. Fleets (troops crossing deep space) and Stargate shots are attacks, not units: see Getting started and Stargates, warheads and shields.

Support: how many you may field

  • Each level of a finished Industrial Zone supports 1 Army.
  • Each level of a finished Spaceport supports 1 Destroyer.
  • With no support to spare, nothing new deploys.
  • Losing support disbands nothing: the units you have stay, and no new one comes until you are back under the limit.
  • Armies and Destroyers each cost 250k, rising with every one you own (500k, 750k, then 1M at the cap), counted separately.

The Hegemon's Destroyers and a pirate's corsair are not limited this way.

Armies

A defensive land unit that patrols your nebula and thins out attacks on you.

  • Deploying. Pick the Army in the build bar (or press 0) and click your own nebula, or open the radial menu on your own nebula and pick Army from the build menu. On a phone or small screen, where the build bar is hidden, the radial menu is the way. The Army sets out from your nearest finished Industrial Zone, walks there through your territory and patrols within 40 tiles of the spot.
  • Moving. Click an Army to select it (Shift-drag to select several, Destroyers included), then click your own nebula to send it there. Armies never leave your territory and never cross deep space.
  • Firing. When the front of an attack on you comes within 40 tiles, an Army fires a shot over the nebula, at most once every 10 seconds. If the attack is still going when the shot lands, 10% of its troops die. Several Armies each fire their own shots.
  • What counts. Every land attack on you: players, Lords, Barons, fleets and Stargate shots once they have landed, and the Hegemon's strikes.
  • Only defence. Armies never attack and never help an attack of yours.
  • Losing one. An Army has no health. It is lost when the tile under it is taken (nobody captures it) or a warhead hits it.
  • Reports. Every hit appears in both players' event panels: you see how many attacking troops your Army killed, the attacker how many they lost.
  • On the map an Army is a pixel star in your colours; in the build bar it shows three rank chevrons. Hotkey 0.

Lords (except on Easy) send their Armies to the front of a land attack and deploy new ones there.

Destroyers

A deep-space warship that patrols, raids and defends.

  • Launching. You need a Spaceport with a harbour on the body of space you click. Pick the Destroyer in the build bar (or press 7) and click deep space, or open the radial menu on deep space and pick Destroyer from the Stargate menu (the gate slot); on a phone the radial menu is the way. The Destroyer launches from your harbour nearest the click and patrols the area.
  • What it does. It captures enemy freighters in open deep space and destroys enemy fleets and Destroyers.
  • Experience. Destroyers level up (up to level 3) by capturing freighters and destroying fleets and Destroyers. Each level adds damage and health.
  • Repair. A Destroyer within 150 tiles of one of your harbours repairs over time; below 75% health it heads home. A spaceport docks as many Destroyers as its level and repairs them faster.
  • Control. Attack-click a Destroyer to select it, then attack-click where it should go. F selects all of your Destroyers; Shift-drag selects several.

The Hegemon's Destroyers need no spaceport: it places them anywhere in deep space and moves them like anyone's. They only defend: they sink fleets bound for the Dark Star, Destroyers that enter the moat, and pirate corsairs anywhere, and they never take freighters. Only the moat repairs them. One posted in the moat patrols it and sails to meet invasion fleets.

The Hegemon may also donate an Army to a challenger whose Industrial Zones have support to spare, for a flat 250k: it becomes that player's own Army.
