Stargates, warheads and shields

Stargates fire troops and launch warheads in Under a Dark Star. Nuclear and Antimatter Warheads, Shield Arrays, and the punitive strike every launch risks.

On this page
  1. Stargates
  2. Warheads
  3. Shield Array

Stargates

A Stargate fires your troops at any tile in range that is not yours.

  • Price. 500k gold, new or per upgrade. The price never climbs and there is no level cap.
  • Range. 180 tiles at level 1, +75 per level. Levels buy range only.
  • Reload. One charge, then a 5-second reload. A troop shot and a warhead launch spend the same charge.
  • The shot takes your attack ratio's share of your troops. It flies an arc and lands like a fleet: it takes the tile and fights outward, and the nebula is kept. If the tile became yours or a friend's in flight, the troops simply join.
  • Nothing stops a shot. No defence intercepts it, and Shield Arrays do not keep it out. A shot in flight can be recalled, troops home whole.
  • The Dark Star. A shot may land on the Dark Star, under the single-invader rule (see Overthrowing the Hegemon). Gates are the late-game road to the throne: they skip the moat.

A gate is the way past a front you cannot break, onto a coast your fleets cannot reach, and onto the Dark Star itself.

Warheads

WarheadPriceBlast radius
Nuclear Warhead1M30 tiles
Antimatter Warhead5M100 tiles
  • Launch. A warhead launches from the nearest ready Stargate whose range covers the target, and spends that gate's charge. With none, there is no launch. A warhead reaches only as far as a gate does, so range upgrades buy reach for warheads too.
  • The blast destroys territory, buildings, ships and fleets, the launcher's own included. Every building in the blast is destroyed outright, all levels.
  • The Dark Star is immune: warheads do nothing to its tiles.
  • The Hegemon fires warheads from the centre of the Dark Star, with no gate and no reload.
  • Warheads cannot be recalled once launched.

The price of firing: punitive strikes

Every launch flags its gate for 30 seconds. In that window the Hegemon may answer with a punitive strike: every living challenger, the launcher included, is levied 10% (Nuclear) or 25% (Antimatter) of the troops they have at home, and the levy lands on the gate as a strike that captures it and razes everything within 34 tiles. When it pops, the gate and every building it captured are destroyed. See The Hegemon.

An AI Hegemon punishes every launch except from very small players.

Shield Array

No other player's warhead can harm anything within 40 tiles of it.

  • Price. 2M gold, one level, no upgrades. 10 seconds to build, and it protects nothing until it is finished.
  • What it stops. When another player's warhead lands, the blast does nothing inside the perimeter: no nebula lost, no troops killed, no building destroyed. The ground outside is cleared as usual. A Nuclear Warhead aimed at the array itself does nothing; an Antimatter Warhead clears only the ring beyond the perimeter.
  • The Hegemon's warheads are kept out like anyone's.
  • What gets through. Troops, not missiles: the Hegemon's strikes and every Stargate shot land inside. Your own warheads are not kept out either.
  • Capture. The array shelters itself, so only conquest or the Hegemon's strike removes it. A captured array shields its new owner.
