Glossary

Every term in Under a Dark Star, from Antimatter Warhead to tyranny - the Hegemon, challengers, nebulae, strikes, bubbles, corsairs and more.

Antimatter Warhead. The large warhead: 5M gold, a 100-tile blast. See Stargates, warheads and shields.

Army. A defensive land unit that patrols your nebula and kills a share of attacking troops. See Armies and Destroyers.

Attack ratio. The share of your troops an attack, fleet or Stargate shot sends.

Baron. A simple AI player. See Lords and Barons.

Beaching. A pirate landing their corsair on a coast to claim a foothold and return as a challenger. See Piracy.

Bubble. The nebula a Hegemon's strike holds while it lasts. Only the struck player may fight it; when it pops, the nebula becomes neutral. See The Hegemon.

Challenger. Every player except the Hegemon.

Corsair. A pirate's one ship. See Piracy.

Dark Star. The supermassive black hole at the centre of every map: a disc of nebula, the Hegemon's seat and the only nebula it can own. See Overthrowing the Hegemon.

Deep space. The space between the nebulae, crossed by fleets, Destroyers and freighters. See Nebulae and deep space.

Defense Post. A building that makes nearby nebula expensive to take. See Buildings.

Destroyer. A deep-space warship launched from a Spaceport's harbour. See Armies and Destroyers.

Embargo. Stopping trade with a player. See Alliances and diplomacy.

Fleet. Troops crossing deep space to land on a coast.

Freighter. A trade ship sailing between two players' Spaceports and paying both. See Trade and the economy.

Gift. A building the Hegemon places or upgrades on a challenger's nebula at its own cost. The host owns it and cannot refuse it.

Harbour. The deep space nearest a Spaceport, where its freighters put to space and its Destroyers launch and repair.

Hegemon. The ruler of the Dark Star: immensely strong, unable to expand. One per round. See The Hegemon.

Hold. The troops a corsair carries; lost if it sinks, sent ashore when it beaches.

Industrial Zone. A building that builds a trade network and sends supply deliveries. Each level supports one Army. See Buildings.

Invader. The one challenger currently allowed to hold Dark Star tiles.

Killing blow. The conquest that takes the Hegemon's last tile. Its owner wins the round.

Lord. A smart AI player placed by the map. See Lords and Barons.

Moat. The ring of deep space around the Dark Star.

Nebula (plural nebulae). The ground of the galaxy: every tile of territory is nebula. See Nebulae and deep space.

Nuclear Warhead. The small warhead: 1M gold, a 30-tile blast.

Overthrow. Eliminating the Hegemon.

Pirate. An eliminated challenger sailing on as a corsair. See Piracy.

Population Center. A building that raises your troop ceiling. See Buildings.

Punitive strike. The Hegemon's answer to a warhead launch: a levy on every challenger that lands on the launching gate. See The Hegemon.

Reign. The consecutive rounds a player holds the same throne. See Thrones and lobbies.

Shadow tax. The Hegemon's income: 3% of everything the challengers earn, taken from nobody.

Shield Array. A building that keeps other players' warheads off everything within 40 tiles. See Stargates, warheads and shields.

Spaceport. A building that sends freighters and launches Destroyers. See Buildings.

Stargate. A building that fires troops at any tile in range and launches warheads. See Stargates, warheads and shields.

Strike. The Hegemon's attack on challenger territory. See The Hegemon.

Supply delivery. A shipment riding an Industrial Zone's trade network, paying gold at every Population Center and Spaceport it passes. See Trade and the economy.

Throne. A public lobby that runs rounds back to back, and the seat of the Hegemon. See Thrones and lobbies.

Trade lane. A straight link between two stations on a trade network.

Tyranny. The rising cost of striking a small player; at the floor they cannot be struck. See The Hegemon.

Void. Impassable space. Nothing crosses it.
