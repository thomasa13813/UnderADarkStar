Thrones and lobbies

Public lobbies in Under a Dark Star are thrones - rooms that run rounds back to back, where the winner keeps the seat. Rooms, reigns, the leaderboard and private lobbies.

Thrones

Public lobbies are thrones: named rooms that run rounds back to back. A room is Free for all or Teams for life.

  • The home page shows the room starting soonest in each mode.
  • The lobby browser lists every throne under Free for all or Teams, with Watch to spectate a running round.

After a round

The winner has 30 seconds to pick the next map (on a team throne, how the teams split too) and to keep the throne or step down. With no answer, the next map is random and the seat opens: the AI holds it, and anyone in the lobby may claim it.

The seat

  • An open seat is first come, first served: claim it in the lobby, or give it up before the start.
  • On a team throne the teams are balanced at the start, on numbers alone: you cannot choose your team, and clans and friends are not grouped. The winner chooses between 2 and 7 teams.
  • If nobody holds it, the AI Hegemon plays.
  • A round needs two humans to start, the Hegemon included.

Rooms

There is one room for each mode, and more open when they fill: once every room of a mode holds three quarters of its 50 players, another opens, and it closes again after a minute with nobody in it. Each room counts down on its own: 60 seconds once two humans are in, at once when it is full. The next round's lobby opens the moment a round ends, and its countdown runs while the winner chooses, but never ends before the winner has had their 30 seconds.

Joining a round in progress

You can join a room whose round is already running, up to its 50 players:

  • During the spawn phase you pick a starting location like everyone else and play as a challenger.
  • After it you come in as a pirate: your first corsair can sail 30 seconds into play. The same goes for anyone who was in the round but never picked a spawn.
  • In the last 3 minutes nobody joins: you watch the end, and you are placed in the room's next lobby.
  • On a team throne you join the team with the fewest players.
  • Leave and come back to the same round and you get your own player back.
  • A player who joined after the start may still land the killing blow, but the round is then void: nobody is credited, and the seat opens for the next round.

Reigns and the leaderboard

  • The throne card counts a reign in consecutive rounds held on the same throne: a slayer starts at 1, and each timer win adds one.
  • On a team throne the seat goes to the player who landed the killing blow, not to the team.
  • The leaderboard records victories (rounds won, whether or not the winner takes the throne), reigns (rounds played as Hegemon) and the longest reign (the most consecutive rounds as Hegemon on one throne).

Private lobbies

In a private lobby the creator seats the Hegemon by clicking a player's crown. New Lobby from the win screen carries the settings over and seats the winner as they arrive.

In a private team lobby, click a team's card to join that team. In Duos, Trios and Quads a full team takes no more players. Anyone who doesn't pick is placed at the start, with clanmates kept together and friends grouped where possible. Lords fill the places left over.
